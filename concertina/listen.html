Which concertina button plays this note?

Play a note on your Anglo C/G concertina and this page shows its pitch and every button that plays it: left or right hand, row, button number, and whether you push or pull the bellows.

Press Start and allow the microphone. The sound is analysed in your browser and never leaves your device. Buttons light up in blue for push and red for pull; buttons 1 and 2 (nearest the player) light up brightest. Use Threshold to ignore background noise.

To find the easiest fingering for a whole tune, use the Concertina Fingering Tool.

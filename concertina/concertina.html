About the Concertina Fingering Tool

The Concertina Fingering Tool finds the easiest fingering for any tune on a 30-button Anglo concertina in C/G. An Anglo concertina is bisonoric: each button plays one note when you push the bellows and another when you pull. On a C/G Anglo, 17 of the 39 notes can be played on more than one button, so a single tune has thousands of possible fingerings.

The tool reads the tune in ABC notation and calculates the route through the buttons that is easiest to play: no finger has to jump to another row, the next note is played on the same button by reversing the bellows where possible, and the c and g rows and the buttons nearest the player are preferred. The search is exact, so the result is the best route under these rules, not an estimate, and it takes well under a second even for long tunes.

How to use it

  1. Copy a tune in ABC notation, for example from The Session or abcnotation.com, and paste it into the ABC notation box.
  2. Press Analyse. The table shows the hand, row, button number and bellows direction (push or pull) for every note; the diagram shows the buttons and good alternatives.
  3. Press Play to hear the tune as written, with note lengths, rests and repeats. Click a note or a table row to start from there.
  4. Open the Practice view on a tablet: sheet music, the current note large in blue (push) or red (pull), and the next two notes.

The tool is free, runs entirely in your browser and uploads nothing. If your instrument differs, download the layout template, edit it and upload your own layout. More in the help and manual, including the full C/G button map.
